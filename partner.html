Collaboration tiers

Platinum partner

Title partner, strategic alignment

  • Primary logo on aircraft and UAV models
  • Logo placement and verbal acknowledgement in official videos and key events
  • Priority placement on flags, banners and standees
  • Featured in reports, submissions and press releases
  • Direct engagement with the core team and leads
  • Association with flagship projects and major events

Gold partner

High visibility, competition presence

  • Secondary logo on selected aircraft models
  • Logo on team uniforms and presentations
  • Shared branding on competition banners and standees
  • Recognition across events and social media
  • Branding during workshops and technical events
  • Presence at national and international competitions

Silver partner

Event and media visibility

  • Logo on team uniforms
  • Logo on presentations and project materials
  • Inclusion on team banners and shared standees
  • Recognition in event communications
  • Social media mentions during competitions
  • Visibility at select AeroBMSCE events

Technical and ecosystem partner

Design, simulation and analysis partner

  • Tools showcased in design and competition reports
  • Credit in technical presentations and submissions
  • Co-branded hands-on workshops
  • Logo on reports, posters and certificates
  • Technical banners and workshop standees
  • Early access to high-performing students
