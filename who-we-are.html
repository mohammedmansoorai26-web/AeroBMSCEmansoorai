Who we are

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, operating from Propel Lab-3.

Established in 2012, the club brings together engineers from 10+ departments to design, build and test advanced aircraft, drones and autonomous systems.

Our vision

We envision AeroBMSCE as a leading aerospace and unmanned systems research group, renowned for design excellence, innovation and competitive success.

We aim to cultivate future leaders in the aerospace and drone industry, establishing a legacy of engineering excellence that inspires students worldwide.
